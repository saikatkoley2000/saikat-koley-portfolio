Battery Energy Storage

The Problem

Launching a New Business Line in Months, Not Years — AI-Accelerated Go-To-Market

Case 02 · Commercial Scale

Tripling a Mature Business in a Market That Barely Grew

Transformer Oil · Eastern India · 2017 to Present

3X Growth vs 20–25% market expansion

Situation

Eastern Region transformer oil was a mature, highly commoditized, price-driven market growing at only 20–25% over the entire decade. Growth had to be aggressively captured from entrenched competitors.

Insight

In commodity-like tenders, the supplier who is easiest to buy from wins. That requires being the first call, having immediate technical answers, and never letting a logistics or payment question go unanswered.

Action

Built disciplined early-stage coverage across utilities, OEMs, EPCs, and plants. Used multi-period consumption data to predict demand before tenders dropped. Learned Power BI to automate dispatch reports, payment alerts, and logistics.

Result

Business tripled while the broader market barely grew. SOTL reached ~75% market share in Eastern India. Over 70 major enterprise accounts now call me first — no proactive asking needed.

Case 03 · Technical Conversion

Converting a Petrochemical Major to Natural Ester Fleet-Wide

Natural Ester Fluids · Industrial · 2022 to Present

100% Adoption 4 consecutive years of ester spec

Situation

When SOTL launched natural ester, no major industrial plant or OEM in Eastern India specified it in higher-rated power transformers. Mineral oil was the unquestioned legacy default despite explosive fire risks.

Insight

Adoption wouldn't start with conservative public utilities. It would start with a safety-critical industrial facility where a transformer fire inside a petrochemical complex is a catastrophe, not an inconvenience.

Action

Leveraged deep ester chemistry background to target Haldia Petrochemicals Limited with sustained techno-commercial presentations on K-class fire safety, extended insulation life, environmental protection, and total lifecycle economics.

Result

Client converted 100% of new transformers to natural ester. For 4 consecutive years, all generator, distribution, and special transformers have been specified with ester — serving as India's premier industrial reference.

Case 04 · High-End Application Engineering

Finding the Application That Justifies a Premium Fluid

Synthetic Ester · Power Conversion · 2024 to Present

2X Growth Projected volume from single export OEM

Situation

Synthetic ester carries a significant upfront price premium over mineral oil and even natural ester, making domestic cost-sensitive buyers hesitant to adopt.

Insight

Never sell a premium fluid on price. Match it to an engineering application where thermal or dielectric failure is unacceptable and international compliance standards are non-negotiable.

Action

Identified a global energy-technology OEM's Chennai facility manufacturing specialised high-voltage DC-DC converter equipment for European and US grids, providing comprehensive fluid qualification datasets.

Result

Customer selected SOTL synthetic ester. Fluid is currently completing final qualification, and on full deployment, this single export programme is projected to double SOTL's synthetic ester business.

Case 05 · Sales Operations & Software

Turning "Ester Costs More" into a Lifecycle Conversation

Decision Tools · Pan-India · 2024 to Present

9 Web Apps Live decision engines used pan-India

Situation

The primary objection across every utility and OEM encounter was upfront price. Frontline sales engineers lacked a fast, verified, credible framework to counter it.

Insight

Utility finance heads don't reject long-term value; they reject unverifiable claims. Hand them the computational model and let them manipulate their own numbers.

Action

Personally engineered a suite of 9 web apps calculating NPV, IRR, payback, and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for both retrofills and new builds, complete with 115 international standards and fire risk data.

Result

Pan-India sales discussions shifted permanently from price-per-litre to lifetime risk mitigation and insurance savings. Established a single source of truth for technical standards across SOTL.

Case 06 · Specification Engineering

Changing Utility Specifications from Mineral Oil to Ester

Cargill India · Eastern Region · 2016 to 2017

23 KL Retrofill CESC breakthrough & APDCL approval

Situation

State power distribution utilities (CESC, WBSEDCL, OPTCL, APDCL) and public works bodies mandated mineral oil in all transformer procurement tenders by default.

Insight

Changing tender clauses takes years. The quickest path to acceptance is proposing fluid retrofilling on critical overloaded assets in high-consequence zones (hospitals, subways, defence).

Action

Conducted pre-tender technical seminars with utility executive engineers, authored formal specification amendments, and proposed turnkey retrofilling as a low-risk validation threshold.

Result

Secured landmark 23 KL synthetic ester retrofill order from CESC. Added Cargill to APDCL approved make list. Paved way for hospital retrofills (CMC & SSKM, NRS) and OPTCL 132/33 kV units.
