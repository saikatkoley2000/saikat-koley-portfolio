## Three Frontiers. One Leader.

<a id="three-frontiers"></a>

Most commercial leaders spend a career mastering one market. I currently head all three of the fastest-moving markets in India's energy transition. Each has different chemistry, different buyers, different standards and different sales cycles.

<!-- Display as three side-by-side cards on desktop, stacked on mobile. -->

### ⚡ Power Transformers
**The backbone of the grid.**

*The problem:* Mineral-oil transformers can catch fire, and when they do, substations, hospitals and industrial plants pay the price.

*What I lead:*
- **Mineral transformer oil** for utilities, OEMs and industry
- **Natural ester** — biodegradable, fire-resistant, made from renewable sources
- **Synthetic ester** — premium fire safety for demanding and export applications

*Proof:* 3X regional growth · ~75% Eastern India share · a petrochemical major converted fully to natural ester

---

### 🔋 Battery Energy Storage (BESS)
**The shock absorber of a renewable grid.**

*The problem:* As India adds solar and wind, it needs massive battery storage. Batteries generate heat, and uncontrolled heat leads to thermal runaway and fire.

*What I lead:*
- **Immersion coolants** that submerge battery cells in a safe dielectric fluid
- **Direct liquid cooling (DLC) coolants** for cold-plate battery systems

*Proof:* QuantiCool launched in-house with zero agency spend · national BESS manufacturer pipeline · supplying BESS manufacturers

---

### 🖥️ Data Centres
**The engine room of AI.**

*The problem:* AI servers run hotter than air cooling can handle. Cooling is now the limit on how much computing a data centre can deliver.

*What I lead:*
- **Immersion coolants** for single-phase immersion-cooled server racks
- **DLC coolants** for direct-to-chip cold-plate cooling

*Proof:* Part of SOTL's position as India's only manufacturer of both immersion and DLC coolants

## Case Studies

<a id="case-studies"></a>

Each case is presented the way consultants present client work: the situation, the insight, the action, and the result.

### Case 1 — Launching a new business with a team of one, powered by AI
**QuantiCool coolants · India · October 2025 to present**

**Situation.** Battery energy storage and AI data centres are creating a new thermal-management market in India. SOTL, positioned as India's only manufacturer of both immersion and direct-liquid-cooling (DLC) coolants, needed to enter a customer base that had never bought from it. I was given the QuantiCool business to lead.

**Insight.** A new B2B product lives or dies on credibility in the first few meetings. Credibility comes from professional collateral, rigorous technical documents and a clear understanding of the customer's problem. Waiting for agencies would have cost months.

**Action.** I built the entire go-to-market toolkit myself, using AI tools and agents:
- **Three product catalogues** for the QuantiCool range
- **A corporate calendar** featuring a series of product and application photographs
- **Banners and posters** for exhibitions and customer events
- **Technical documentation**, including safety data sheets, certificates of analysis and corrosion test reports for QuantiCool 520EV and 650 ES
- **A BESS thermal-management feasibility analysis** and a **BESS fire-incident database** to frame the safety case for immersion cooling
- **The QuantiCool BESS Outreach Tracker**, a full commercial intelligence application mapping every BESS manufacturer in India

**Result.**
- A complete launch kit delivered **in-house, with zero agency involvement**.
- QuantiCool exhibited at **The Battery Show 2025** and is exhibiting at the **2nd Annual Conference on BESS for Utilities** (New Delhi, October 2026).
- SOTL has begun supplying BESS manufacturers, with a structured national pipeline from first contact to specification.

### Case 2 — Tripling a mature business in a market that barely grew
**Transformer oil · Eastern India · 2017 to present**

**Situation.** The Eastern Region transformer oil market was mature, price-driven and growing at 20–25% over the period. Growth had to come from competitors.

**Insight.** In a commodity-like market, the supplier who is easiest to buy from wins. That means being the first call, having the answer ready, and never letting a delivery or payment question go unanswered.

**Action.**
- Built disciplined early-stage coverage across utilities, OEMs, EPCs and industrial plants.
- Used multi-period dispatch, payment and consumption data to predict demand rather than wait for enquiries.
- Learned Power BI three years ago and automated dispatch reporting, payment reminders and vehicle tracking for every account.

**Result.**
- Business grew **3X** while the market grew 20–25%.
- SOTL reached **~75% share** of the Eastern Region.
- **70+ customers** now call me when they need material; I no longer need to ask.

### Case 3 — Converting a petrochemical major to natural ester, fleet-wide
**Natural ester fluids · Industrial · 2022 to present**

**Situation.** When SOTL launched natural ester, no major customer or OEM in Eastern India used it in higher-rated power transformers. Mineral oil was the unquestioned default.

**Insight.** Ester adoption would not start with utilities, which change slowly. It would start with a safety-critical industrial site where a transformer fire is a catastrophe, not an inconvenience.

**Action.** Using my earlier ester experience at Cargill, I moved immediately after launch. I targeted **Haldia Petrochemicals Limited** with a sustained programme of presentations and technical discussions covering fire safety, ageing, environmental performance and lifecycle cost.

**Result.**
- The customer moved **entirely from mineral oil to natural ester** for every new project.
- For **four consecutive years**, all new generator, distribution and special-application transformers have been specified with natural ester.
- The account is now a reference case for ester adoption in Indian heavy industry.

---

### Case 4 — Finding the application that justifies a premium fluid
**Synthetic ester · Power conversion · 2024 to present**

**Situation.** Synthetic ester is a premium product, and its price makes it a hard sell in a cost-sensitive domestic market.

**Insight.** A premium fluid should not compete on price. It should be matched to an application where failure is unacceptable and international standards are strict.

**Action.** I studied the product in depth and identified **a global energy-technology OEM's Chennai facility** building specialised DC-DC converter equipment for Europe and the United States. Through a series of presentations and technical discussions, the customer selected SOTL's synthetic ester.

**Result.**
- The fluid is in qualification testing with the customer.
- On approval, this single programme is projected to **double SOTL's synthetic ester business**.

---

### Case 5 — Turning "ester costs more" into a lifecycle conversation
**Decision tools · Pan-India · 2024 to present**

**Situation.** The most common objection to ester fluids is upfront price. Sales teams lacked a quick, credible way to answer it.

**Insight.** Utility buyers do not reject value; they reject claims they cannot verify. Give them the model, and let them run their own numbers.

**Action.** I built a suite of nine web applications that together make the market, economic, technical and safety case for ester fluids and coolants. The economic tool calculates NPV, IRR, payback and total cost of ownership for retrofill and new-fill projects, and is maintained with a colleague for use by teams across every region.

**Result.**
- Sales conversations moved from price per litre to lifetime value and risk.
- A single source of truth for standards, competitor benchmarks and regulations across the company.

---

### Case 6 — Changing utility specifications from mineral oil to ester
**Cargill India · Eastern Region · 2016 to 2017**

**Situation.** Utilities and public buyers wrote mineral oil into specifications by default.

**Action.** Pre-tender engagement, technical presentations, specification changes and retrofilling as a low-risk entry point.

**Result.**
- **23 KL synthetic ester retrofill order** from CESC.
- Company added to **APDCL's approved make list**.
- Ester-filled transformers considered for hospital and defence projects, including 8 × 10 MVA retrofills at a major Kolkata medical college; OPTCL moved toward 3 × 20 MVA, 132/33 kV ester-filled units.
